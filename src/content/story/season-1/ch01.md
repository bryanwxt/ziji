## Setup

### Page 1
@scene hdb-morning
It was Saturday {早上|morning}. Truffle lay on his windowsill. The {太阳|sun} warmed his tummy.
He was the happiest cat on the eighth floor.
> Truffle: Purrrrr. Nobody disturb me. I am busy being warm.
Then, from the corridor, came a sound. *SHHHHHHHH.*

### Page 2
@scene hdb-morning
A tall man in a long purple coat was pushing a brass machine. It had a huge funnel and a glass belly.
> Hush: SHHHHHH! Silence is GOLDEN!
The funnel sucked. Words peeled off everything like stickers and swirled into the belly.
The sunshine on Truffle's tummy went grey. And cold.

### Page 3
@scene hdb-morning
Truffle leapt off the windowsill after him. Behind him, the {门|door} of his {家|home} swung shut. Click.
Hush tripped on his coat. He crashed into the washing. He ran for the stairs with grey {衣服|clothes} on his head.
Truffle jabbed the {电梯|lift} button. Nothing. It had gone grey. It had forgotten how to move.
> Truffle: Come back with my SUN!

### Page 4
@scene hdb-morning
Truffle pushed his door. It had forgotten how to open, too.
You came down the corridor and stopped. On the grey door, faint ghost words glowed.
> Truffle: What are you staring at? It's grey.
> Truffle: You can SEE words? I can't read. I'm a cat.
> Truffle: You're a Word-Keeper! Read them back and it'll open! My {床|bed} is in my {房间|room}!

## Payoff

### Page 1
@scene hdb-morning
You read the ghost words out loud, one by one. You knew what each one meant.
[rescued] Far below, the machine hiccupped. Every word you read shot back up the stairs and zoomed home.
The door clicked open. Truffle raced inside and leapt onto his soft, warm bed.
Out in the corridor, something went DING!

### Page 2
@scene hdb-morning
The sunshine came back to the windowsill. Truffle posed in it like a king.
> Truffle: Hmph. Well done, partner. I knew it would work.
He rolled over to warm his tummy. He rolled right off the windowsill. BONK.
> Truffle: I meant to do that.
Then he went for a drink. The kitchen tap was still grey. Not one drop came out.
