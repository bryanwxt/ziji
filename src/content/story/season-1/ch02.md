## Setup

### Page 1
@scene hdb-morning
Truffle hopped onto the {桌子|table}. He glared at the grey tap. Not one drop of {水|water} came out.
> Truffle: I'm SO thirsty! There's a ghost word on it, isn't there?
> Truffle: Just tell me how the words SOUND. Don't explain! I'm VERY good at this.
You started to explain. Truffle's paw covered your mouth. So you told him only the sounds.

### Page 2
@scene hdb-morning
Truffle took a deep breath.
> Truffle: SHUǏ!
Nothing. He shouted louder. His whiskers wobbled.
> Truffle: SHUǏ!! SHUUUUǏ!!!
Nothing. He tried the grey {毛巾|towel} too.
> Truffle: MÁOJĪN!
He leapt up to shake it. The towel slid off its hook, right onto his head. Flump.

### Page 3
@scene hdb-morning
> Truffle: Why isn't it WORKING? I said it PERFECTLY!
He crawled under his grey {被子|blanket} to sulk. It was stiff as a board. BONK.
*SHHHHHHHH!* Hush whizzed past the front door again, and down the stairs.
Your {鞋|shoes} on the doormat went grey. They stuck to the floor!

## Payoff

### Page 1
@scene hdb-morning
You told Truffle what each word meant.
> Truffle: SHUǏ means WATER? Water to drink?
He said it again. This time he knew what it meant.
[rescued] The ghost words glowed. One by one, they floated home.
WHOOSH! Water blasted out of the tap, right in Truffle's face.
> Truffle: Hmph. A word only works if you understand it. I knew that.

### Page 2
@scene hdb-morning
Cats don't {洗|wash} with water. Truffle just did. All of him.
He shook himself like a wet mop. SPLOSH! Your {袜子|socks} got soaked.
He dried off on the towel, then flopped onto his blanket. Both were soft again.
He combed his whiskers with a {牙刷|toothbrush}. Your shoes let go of the floor.
> Truffle: Breakfast! Last one down is a soggy biscuit!
