## Setup

### Page 1
@scene hdb-morning
Truffle wriggled back in through the cat flap. You followed. (It was a very big cat flap.)
Inside, everything was still grey. The {桌子|table} was grey. The {被子|blanket} on his bed was grey and flat, like a pancake.
> Truffle: Right. Here's my plan. You read. I fix.

### Page 2
@scene hdb-morning
Truffle hopped up to the kitchen tap and turned it. No {水|water} came out. Not one drop.
Truffle couldn't see words. So he asked you how to *say* this one. You told him the sound. Just the sound.
Truffle shouted it, very loudly.
> Truffle: SHUǏ! SHUǏ!!
The tap stayed grey.

### Page 3

> Truffle: MÁOJĪN! MÁOJĪN!! MÁÁÁOJĪN!!!

> Truffle: Word-Keeper… what does it actually MEAN?

## Go
Truffle's tummy rumbled. He ignored it. Mostly.
> Truffle: Food later. First, tell me what the words MEAN, Word-Keeper.

## Payoff

### Page 1
@scene hdb-morning
So you told him. A towel is for drying. Water is for drinking and washing.
The moment Truffle understood, the grey began to melt.
[rescued] One by one, the words floated home, and the flat filled with colour.
> Truffle: Ah. Of course. A word only works if you understand it. I knew that.

### Page 2
@scene hdb-morning
Truffle found his {牙刷|toothbrush}. He did not {洗|wash} his face. Cats lick.
By the door sat two tiny {鞋|shoes} and a pair of {袜子|socks}. Truffle had "borrowed" them from next door.
Then his tummy rumbled. From the void deck far below came the smell of eggs and toast.
Truffle's ears shot straight up.
