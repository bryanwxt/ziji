Except for one corner.

### Page 2
@scene hdb-voiddeck
In the corner was a little kopitiam, bright as a lantern. Steam curled up from a pot of {汤|soup}.
Behind the counter stood a small green dragon in round glasses. She was everybody's {奶奶|granny}. Everybody called her Granny Dragon.
> Truffle: Careful. She only speaks Mandarin.
> Truffle: I understand all of it. Well. Most of it. Some.

## Granny

龙奶奶: 你们饿了吗？ | Are you hungry?
龙奶奶: 小心，汤很热！ | Careful, the soup is hot!

## Go
Granny Dragon put down plates of {鸡蛋|eggs} and toast, and a saucer of warm {牛奶|milk} for Truffle.
Truffle ate like a vacuum cleaner. Then he looked around at the grey void deck.
> Truffle: Mmf. While she cooks, we fix. Bring these words back, Word-Keeper.
