## Setup

### Page 1
@scene hdb-voiddeck
DING! The lift doors slid open at the void deck. Everything down here was grey too.
Truffle hopped onto a grey {椅子|chair}. It forgot it was a chair and folded flat. FLOMP.
> Truffle: Hmph! That chair was RUDE.
Then his nose twitched. Soup!

### Page 2
@scene hdb-voiddeck
In one corner was a little kopitiam. Everything in it was grey, except one pot of {汤|soup}. It glowed like a lantern.
Behind the counter stood a small green dragon in round glasses. She was everybody's {奶奶|granny}. They called her Granny Dragon.
How did her soup still work? Nobody knew.

### Page 3
@scene hdb-voiddeck
An auntie wanted two {包子|buns}. But the buns in the steamer were grey lumps. She tapped one. TOK TOK. Like a stone!
Truffle's breakfast sat on the counter. A grey {鸡蛋|egg}. Grey {面包|bread}, hard as a brick.
> Truffle: My BREAKFAST!
> Truffle: Psst. Granny Dragon only speaks Mandarin. I understand all of it. Well. Most of it. Some.

## Granny
Granny Dragon leaned close and spoke slowly.
龙奶奶: 你们饿了吗？ | Are you hungry?
龙奶奶: 小心，汤很热！ | Careful, the soup is hot!

## Go
She slid two bowls of soup across the counter. Truffle gulped his in one go.
His fur shot straight up. Steam came out of his ears.
> Truffle: HOT! HOT! HOT! Why didn't anybody WARN me?
> Truffle: Word-Keeper, you can see the food's ghost words. Read them back. I want my egg!

## Payoff

### Page 1
@scene hdb-voiddeck
You read the ghost words out loud. You knew what each one meant.
[rescued] All around the void deck, words popped back. Colour came back with them.
The buns puffed up, soft and steaming. The auntie took a big bite and grinned.
The chairs stood up straight. Truffle sat on one. It held.

### Page 2
@scene hdb-voiddeck
Truffle cracked his egg and gobbled his bread. Granny Dragon poured him warm {牛奶|milk}. She gave you a packet of {饭|rice} with chicken.
Then, from far away, came a howl.
> Dog: AROOO! By the beard of Zeus! I'm stuck in the playground!
Granny Dragon nodded and pointed her spoon. That way!
> Truffle: The playground! Come on, partner!
