# One River — Gemini prompts

Paste one whole prompt per world. Every painting must be square, full-bleed, with water leaving at the lower right, an open lower-left foreground, a calm centre, and no people, animals, boats or text. These rules come from the spec, docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-13-ziji-one-river-design.md §3.2.

## 1. 家门口 (home)
A square 1:1 landscape painting for a children's storybook, no people, no animals, no boats, no text, full-bleed edge to edge, no border, no frame, no margin. Early sunrise, pale gold light. Cream-and-terracotta Singapore HDB blocks behind a quiet concrete canal lined with rain trees, a footbridge in the distance, a grassy bank in front. The canal comes from the far distance near the centre of the horizon, winds toward the viewer, and flows out at the lower-right corner. The lower-left foreground is open, flat grassy ground. Painterly gouache with chunky, visible brush dabs and simplified, stylised shapes, not photoreal. A big high-key sky with soft pastel clouds in pink, lavender, peach and mint. Gentle bloom and soft glow, distance fading into warm haze, shadows cool blue-violet, never black. The centre of the picture is calm and uncluttered. Calm, cosy, slightly magical.

## 7. 海湾 (cove)
A square 1:1 landscape painting for a children's storybook, no people, no animals, no boats in the water, no text, full-bleed edge to edge, no border, no frame, no margin. A sandy cove at dusk under a peach-and-violet sky: a white lighthouse on a rocky headland on the right, an old wooden shipwreck lying half-buried in the sand, palm trees leaning over the beach, and gentle waves. The river comes from the far distance near the centre of the horizon, widens as it winds toward the viewer, and flows out into the sea at the lower-right corner. The lower-left foreground is open, flat sand. Painterly gouache with chunky, visible brush dabs and simplified, stylised shapes, not photoreal. A big high-key dusk sky with soft pastel clouds in pink, lavender, peach and mint, and the first warm glow from the lighthouse. Gentle bloom and soft glow, the far sea fading into violet haze, shadows cool blue-violet, never black. The centre of the picture is calm and uncluttered. Adventurous but cosy, slightly magical.

## 8. 银河 (milkyway)
A square 1:1 landscape painting for a children's storybook, no people, no animals, no boats, no text, full-bleed edge to edge, no border, no frame, no margin. A calm open sea at deep night under a vast starry sky. A shimmering river of silver light runs across the water from the lower-right corner, winds away from the viewer toward the centre of the horizon, and there rises up into the sky to become the Milky Way. A crescent moon, soft glowing clouds, and gentle sparkles on the water. The lower-left foreground is a flat, open rock ledge at the water's edge. Painterly gouache with chunky, visible brush dabs and simplified, stylised shapes, not photoreal. Navy, violet and silver with touches of pastel pink and mint in the glowing clouds. Gentle bloom and soft starlight glow, shadows deep blue-violet, never black. The centre of the picture is calm and uncluttered. Peaceful, wondrous, magical.
