# Season 1 — *The Silent Street* (outline)

**Words:** 一上, 一下, 二上 (catch-up). Chapters 1–6 use only 一上/一下 slot words; the season moves into 二上 after that.
**Bible:** `docs/story/bible.md`. **Checked by:** `npx tsx scripts/story/check.ts`.

**The season in brief:**
- One sunny Saturday, Professor Hush wheels his Hush-o-Matic into Truffle's HDB block and sucks the words out of it. Doors stick, the lift stops and the lights won't come on.
- Truffle discovers the one person who can still see words: the Word-Keeper.
- Fed and quietly guided by Granny Dragon at the void-deck kopitiam, they rescue a crew one by one:
  - Dog, from a playground whose gate has forgotten itself;
  - Pig, from a hawker centre where every dish has lost its name;
  - Ox, from a wet market where 买 and 卖 have swapped places.
- The crew restores the school, the market and the hawker centre. They learn the magic's rules the hard way, and notice that Hush flinches whenever Granny Dragon speaks.
- They track the machine to a word-store under the void deck, where Ox opens the lock by taking an instruction literally. They find an old photo of a boy and his grandmother.
- The block's words come home, but Hush escapes with his machine towards the MRT station.

**Where the rules and hints land:**
- **Rule 1** (a word works only if understood): first shown in ch 2.
- **Rule 2** (tones): ch 8 (买/卖), with a callback in ch 14 (汤/糖).
- **Rule 3** (parts combine): ch 12–13.
- **Hint 1:** ch 14.
- **Hint 2:** ch 16.
