## Setup

### Page 1

Truffle lay on his windowsill on the eighth floor. The {太阳|sun} warmed his tummy.
> Truffle: Perfect. Nothing will happen today. Nothing ever happens here.
Then, from the corridor, came a sound. *SHHHHHHHH.*

### Page 2
@scene hdb-morning
A tall man in a long purple coat was pushing a brass machine down the corridor. It had a huge funnel and a glass belly.
> Hush: SHHHHHH! Silence is GOLDEN!
The funnel sucked. Words peeled off the walls like stickers. They swirled round and round inside the glass belly.

### Page 3
@scene hdb-morning

> Truffle: Hey! That's MY bed!
Outside, the {衣服|clothes} on the bamboo poles went grey too. They flapped like sad ghosts.
Truffle ran to the {门|door}. It would not open.
