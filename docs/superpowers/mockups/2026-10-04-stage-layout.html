How should a lesson sit in Truffle's world?

Three stage layouts, each shown on iPad landscape (left) and iPad upright (right), at the moment after a right answer. The backyard art is the depth-and-story-details sketch. Click the one you prefer.

A — The stage

The whole world fills the screen; the lesson is a paper card in a fixed place; Truffle always stands in the same spot on his ground with his bubble; feedback docks as one full-width sheet at the bottom. Most immersive — you feel inside Truffle's world.

B — Storybook spread

Like an open picture book: the left page is the illustration with Truffle in his world, the right page is the lesson on paper. Upright, the picture sits above the page. Calmest for reading; the world never sits behind the answers.

C — World on top, page below

The world is a wide band across the top with Truffle in it; the lesson sits on a plain paper page below it. Simple and very clear, but the world is smaller.
